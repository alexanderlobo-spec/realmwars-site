Realm Wars é um jogo de damas brasileiras para iPhone e iPad, com as regras oficiais: captura obrigatória, lei da maioria, dama voadora e coroação na última fileira. About the app

Realm Wars is a Brazilian checkers game for iPhone and iPad with the official rules: mandatory capture, the majority rule, flying kings, and crowning on the last row. Play against the AI, two players on one device, or online in turns through Game Center, and choose from three realms, twelve armies, and six battlefields. In Portuguese, English, Spanish, and French.

Free. No ads, no purchases, no subscription, no donations. The app accepts no payments of any kind.

The app is in private testing through TestFlight. When it is available on the App Store, the link will appear here.

Support

Questions, suggestions, or a problem in the game? Write to realmwars@ponte.day. Replies in Portuguese, English, Spanish, or French.

To help solve it faster, include your device model, the iOS version, and what happened (the app version is shown at the bottom of Settings).
